01 / PROBLEM STATEMENT

The Nearest-Gene Assumption Causes Systematic Phase II Failures

Legacy Paradigm (Nearest-Gene Heuristic)

1D Linear Proximity Shortcut

Standard GWAS post-processing assigns a non-coding variant to the nearest transcription start site. In over 70% of non-coding loci, 3D chromosome conformation capture (Hi-C / Micro-C) proves enhancers loop over intermediate genes to regulate distant promoters hundreds of kilobases away.

Failure Mode: $1B+ wasted developing molecules against non-causal bystander genes.

03 / METHODOLOGY

The 4-Stage Discovery Architecture

STAGE 01

Bayesian Fine-Mapping

Sum of Single Effects (SuSiE) over LD reference panels isolates single causal variants with Posterior Inclusion Probability (PIP ≥ 0.95).

STAGE 02

Digital Footprinting

TOBIAS base-pair footprinting quantifies chromatin occupancy, filtering out passive transcription factor motifs.

STAGE 03

Stereospecific Phasing

Evaluates 10.5 bp helical pitch distances between pioneer factors and recruited co-activators to verify 3D enhanceosome formation.

STAGE 04

qChIP-MS Validation

Quantitative chromatin pull-down mass spectrometry verifies allele-specific protein complex recruitment in primary tissue.
